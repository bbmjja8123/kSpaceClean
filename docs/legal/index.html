Privacy Policy

kWatch by Kraftly · Last updated: 2026-08-01

kWatch is built around a single promise: your Mac's data stays on your Mac. This policy explains what kWatch does and does not collect, how the data you see in the menu bar is processed, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

kWatch is a macOS menu-bar application that displays real-time system metrics (CPU, memory, disk, network, temperature, fan speed, battery, and process activity). All metric data is read from your Mac using Apple public APIs and is processed locally. kWatch does not run a backend service, does not transmit telemetry, and does not require Full Disk Access.

  • No account required. kWatch works without sign-in.
  • No remote servers. Metric data never leaves your device.
  • No advertising or tracking. kWatch integrates no ad SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics. Crash data stays on-device unless you choose to share it.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the kWatch application distributed on the Mac App Store under the bundle identifier app.kraftly.kwatch and to the optional companion targets shipped in the same app bundle (Widget extension, Live Activity, and Shortcuts integration). It does not cover other Kraftly applications such as kSpaceClean, kDupe, or kFresh, which maintain their own privacy documentation.

3. Data we do not collect

kWatch does not collect, transmit, or sell the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • Your Apple ID, Apple Account email, or any sign-in credentials.
  • Your precise or coarse location.
  • Your contacts, calendars, reminders, photos, or messages.
  • Your browsing history, microphone audio, or camera input.
  • Health, fitness, or biometric data.
  • Usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking signals.
  • Any system metric shown in the menu bar.

4. Data stored locally on your Mac

The only persistent storage kWatch uses is the standard macOS Application Support directory and the shared App Group container. The following data is written locally:

Data Purpose Location Retention
System metric history (CPU, memory, disk, network) Display trend charts and compute alerts App Group Core Data store 7 or 30 days (user-configurable)
Alert log Show the most recent triggered alerts App Group Core Data store 90 days
User preferences (thresholds, refresh interval, theme) Restore your configuration across launches App Group UserDefaults Until you change it or uninstall
MetricKit diagnostics (crash and hang reports) Help diagnose stability issues if you report them ~/Library/Application Support/kWatch/diagnostics/ 7 days, capped at 10 MB

All of the above lives on your Mac in your user account. None of it is transmitted to Kraftly or to any third party automatically.

5. System APIs kWatch uses

kWatch reads system information exclusively through Apple public APIs. The following are used at runtime, all in read-only fashion:

API What kWatch reads Why
sysctl / host_processor_info / host_statistics64 CPU usage, load average, memory statistics, boot time Core CPU and memory metric cards
statfs Per-volume disk usage and free space Disk usage metric card
getifaddrs (BSD sockets) Per-interface byte and packet counters Network throughput metric card
libproc (proc_listallpids, proc_pidinfo) Process name, PID, CPU/memory usage for the top-N list Top processes panel (Pro feature)
IOKit AppleSMC CPU and GPU temperature, fan RPM Temperature and fan metric cards (Pro feature)
IOKit IOAppleBattery / IOPS Battery cycle count, current capacity, charging state Battery metric card

kWatch does not install kernel extensions, system daemons, or privileged helpers. It does not modify any system file and does not write to any location outside your user account.

6. Entitlements and permissions

kWatch runs inside the App Sandbox. The application declares only the minimum entitlements needed to read system metrics:

  • com.apple.security.app-sandbox — required for all Mac App Store apps.
  • com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-only — only if you choose to open a diagnostic report from Finder.

kWatch does not request Full Disk Access. There is no folder you need to add to the Privacy & Security pane for kWatch to function. The macOS permission prompts you may see relate to standard Apple frameworks (for example, Notifications) and can be declined without affecting metric monitoring.

7. In-app purchases and the App Store

kWatch offers a one-time Pro upgrade. Payment is processed by Apple through StoreKit; Kraftly never sees your credit card number or Apple Account password. Apple provides Kraftly with a transaction identifier, the product identifier, and the date of purchase so the Pro entitlement can be restored on your devices. You can revoke the Pro entitlement at any time by restoring purchases or by deleting the app.

A Pro subscription is not offered in the v1 release. If subscription pricing is introduced in a future release, this policy will be updated and a renewal-disclosure sheet will be presented in the Paywall before purchase, in accordance with App Store Review Guideline 3.1.2(a).

8. Diagnostics and crash reports

kWatch uses Apple's MetricKit framework to record crash and hang payloads on your Mac. MetricKit data is written to local files inside ~/Library/Application Support/kWatch/diagnostics/ and is never uploaded automatically.

If you choose to Report a problem from the Settings → About pane, the app prepares a diagnostic bundle containing the most recent 24 hours of MetricKit data, a snapshot of current metrics, and your macOS version. The bundle is attached to a pre-filled mailto: link so you can review and edit the contents before sending. The diagnostic bundle is only transmitted if you explicitly press Send in your mail client.

The diagnostic bundle may contain the names of running processes and the application names of any process visible in the top-N list. It does not contain the contents of any file, your Apple ID, your IP address, or any personal identifier.

9. Contacting support

If you email support@kraftly.app, Kraftly will receive the contents of your message, including any attachments and any diagnostic bundle you choose to include. We use this information only to respond to your request, to troubleshoot kWatch on your Mac, and to improve the product. We do not use support emails for marketing.

10. Data retention and deletion

Retention windows enforced by kWatch:

  • Metric history — 7 or 30 days (your choice), then permanently deleted.
  • Alert log — 90 days, then permanently deleted.
  • MetricKit diagnostics — 7 days, oldest entries purged when total size exceeds 10 MB.
  • User preferences — retained until you change them.

Uninstalling kWatch from the Finder or Launchpad removes the app binary. Local data written by kWatch lives in your user library and is not removed automatically. To erase all kWatch data, choose Settings → Advanced → Reset all data before uninstalling, or manually delete the ~/Library/Application Support/kWatch/ folder and the kWatch entry inside the App Group container after uninstalling.

11. Third-party SDKs

kWatch does not bundle any third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services. All features are implemented with Apple frameworks: StoreKit, MetricKit, ActivityKit, AppIntents, CoreSpotlight, IOKit, and Core Data.

12. Children's privacy

kWatch is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). kWatch does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because kWatch does not collect personal information from anyone, no special handling applies.

13. International users

kWatch does not transfer your data to servers located in another country, because kWatch does not have servers. All processing happens on the device you are using. The privacy practices described in this policy are designed to satisfy the requirements of the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the United Kingdom's Data Protection Act, Brazil's LGPD, and the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.

Your rights. Where local law grants you rights over personal data held about you (access, deletion, portability, or objection), those rights are satisfied automatically by the local-only design of kWatch. You can exercise them directly through the Settings pane or by uninstalling the app.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, kWatch will display an in-app notice on next launch and update the Last updated date above. Non-material edits (typo fixes, clarifications) will be reflected in the Last updated date only. Previous versions of this policy are available on request from privacy@kraftly.app.
