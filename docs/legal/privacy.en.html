kWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Summary

kWatch is a menu-bar system monitor. All metric readings, alert thresholds, and Pro entitlements live on your Mac. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Data kWatch does NOT collect

  • Identity (name, email, phone)
  • Location
  • Contacts, calendars, photos
  • Health data
  • Browsing history
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports
  • Diagnostic data

What kWatch accesses on your Mac

kWatch reads the following system signals locally to display metrics:

  • CPU, memory, disk, network counters — read via host_processor_info, sysctl, proc_listpids, getifaddrs.
  • Temperature, fan RPM — read via the Apple System Management Controller (SMC) through Apple's IO-kit APIs. Available only on hardware that exposes these sensors; macOS may require Full Disk Access.
  • Battery state — read via IOPSCopyPowerSourcesInfo.

These reads happen on-device. kWatch never opens network connections to transmit readings.

Permissions kWatch requests

  • Full Disk Access — required to read protected system paths on macOS 13+. Declined by default; you can grant it from System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access.
  • Notifications — used for threshold alerts. Declined by default.

Pro subscription

The Pro upgrade is processed by Apple via StoreKit. We never see your Apple ID, payment method, or billing details. Apple handles all subscription state; we receive only a single verified entitlement flag.

Children's privacy

kWatch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new "Last updated" date above. Continued use of kWatch after a change constitutes acceptance.
